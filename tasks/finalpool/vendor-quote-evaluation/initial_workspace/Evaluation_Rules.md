# RFQ-2025-031 - Evaluation Rules

## 1. Requirement

- RFQ number: RFQ-2025-031
- Item: ergonomic mesh-back task chairs for the Riverside office expansion
- Quantity required: 150 units
- Delivery address: Riverside site
- Planned award date: 15 October 2025
- Budget ceiling: USD 60,000.00 (Total Evaluated Cost)
- Maximum lead time: 45 calendar days after receipt of purchase order
- Minimum warranty on the chair mechanism: 5 years (60 months)
- Required certifications: the vendor must hold a valid ISO 9001 certificate on the planned award date (see Supplier_Due_Diligence_Notes.docx), and the quoted chair must be BIFMA X5.1 certified (see the quotation)
- Evaluation currency: USD
- Exchange rate: EUR 1.00 = USD 1.08 (fixed for this evaluation)

## 2. Which quotation counts

- Every vendor is identified by the three-letter vendor code assigned in the RFQ. The code appears on the first line of each quotation and of each due-diligence entry.
- If a vendor submitted more than one quotation, only the most recent one (latest quotation date) is evaluated. Earlier revisions are disregarded completely and do not receive an evaluation sheet of their own.
- Vendors are evaluated on the quotation as written. Do not assume corrections, extensions or clarifications that are not in the documents.

## 3. Total Evaluated Cost

Compute the Total Evaluated Cost for every vendor, including vendors that turn out to be non-compliant:

1. Goods subtotal = unit price x quantity quoted. Use the quantity the vendor actually quoted, even if it is less than the 150 units required.
2. Volume discount: apply the vendor's stated percentage to the goods subtotal only if the quantity quoted meets the stated threshold ("N units or more" includes exactly N). Otherwise apply no discount. Discounts never apply to freight.
3. Add freight, unless the quotation states that freight is included in the unit price.
4. If the quotation is in EUR, convert the resulting total to USD at the fixed exchange rate above.
5. Round the final figure to two decimals (cents).

## 4. Mandatory criteria

A quotation is compliant only if all six criteria are met. Check them in the order below and, for every criterion that fails, record the reason phrase exactly as written in the table:

| # | Criterion | Reason phrase when failed |
|---|-----------|---------------------------|
| 1 | The quantity quoted is the full 150 units | Partial quantity |
| 2 | The lead time is 45 calendar days or less | Lead time exceeds 45 days |
| 3 | The warranty on the mechanism is 5 years (60 months) or more | Warranty below 5 years |
| 4 | The vendor holds a valid ISO 9001 certificate on the planned award date and the chair is BIFMA X5.1 certified | Missing or expired certification |
| 5 | The quotation is still valid on the planned award date (a validity stated as a number of days counts from the quotation date) | Quote expires before award date |
| 6 | The Total Evaluated Cost does not exceed the budget ceiling | Exceeds budget ceiling |

"Mandatory Criteria Met" is written as the number of criteria met out of six, e.g. `6/6` or `4/6`.

## 5. Decision

- Compliant vendors (6/6) are ranked by Total Evaluated Cost, lowest first. Ties are broken by the shorter lead time, then alphabetically by vendor name.
- Decision values:
  - `Award` for the top-ranked compliant vendor;
  - `Compliant - Not Selected` for every other compliant vendor;
  - `Disqualified` for every vendor that fails at least one criterion.
- The "Disqualification Reasons" cell lists the reason phrases of all failed criteria, in criterion order, separated by `; `. For compliant vendors write `None`.
